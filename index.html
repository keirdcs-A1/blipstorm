Support and privacy information for Blipstorm on iPhone.

Contact Blipstorm support

Email ktosint98@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version from Settings. Never send passwords or payment-card details.

Common questions

I bought something, but it is not showing.

Open Blipstorm, go to Settings and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed into the Apple Account used for the purchase and have an internet connection. You can also restore from a purchase-offer screen.

Blipstorm+, Neon Pack and Retro Pack are one-time purchases. If restoring does not resolve the issue, contact support with the product name and a description of what happened.

How do I request a refund?

Apple processes App Store purchases and refund requests. Visit Apple's Report a Problem service and sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress and settings are saved on your device. Blipstorm does not run its own cloud-save service. Settings > Reset Progress erases local coins, stars and progress. Be careful when deleting the app or changing devices.

Purchased products can be restored through Apple. Local game progress and purchases are separate.

How do leaderboards work?

Blipstorm uses Apple's Game Center for leaderboards. Check your Game Center sign-in in iPhone Settings if scores or leaderboards are unavailable. You can play without signing into Game Center.

How do I change sound, music or vibration?

Open Settings inside Blipstorm to switch sound effects, music and haptics on or off.

I found a bug.

Email support with the steps that caused it, what you expected, and what happened. An optional screenshot can help. Please remove personal information before sending it.
