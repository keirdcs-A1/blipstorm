Blipstorm

Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Blipstorm is an iPhone game developed by Keir Thompson in the United Kingdom. For privacy questions or requests, email ktosint98@gmail.com. Keir Thompson is responsible for personal information handled directly for Blipstorm support.

Which version does this cover?

This policy covers Blipstorm and this support website. Blipstorm uses local game storage, Apple's Game Center, Apple's purchase services and optional Google rewarded ads.

1. Progress and settings on your device

Blipstorm saves game progress, coins, unlocked items, settings and a cache of purchase entitlements on your device. These support gameplay, remember preferences and make purchased features available. Blipstorm does not operate its own player-account or cloud-save server, and does not send your complete local save to the developer.

You can reset local progress in the game's Settings. Deleting the app removes its local app data, subject to your device's backup and restore settings. Resetting local progress does not cancel an Apple purchase or erase Game Center records.

2. Game Center

If you are signed into Game Center, Blipstorm submits scores and campaign-star totals to Apple's leaderboards and retrieves player display names and scores for leaderboard features. Your Game Center nickname and scores may be visible to other players. Apple handles Game Center account information under its own policies. You can play without Game Center and manage its sign-in and sharing choices in iPhone Settings.

Apple's Game Center privacy information

3. Purchases

Apple processes payments for Blipstorm+, Neon Pack and Retro Pack. Blipstorm uses StoreKit purchase and entitlement information to verify ownership, enable items and restore purchases. The developer does not receive your payment-card number from the app. Apple may provide the developer with sales, financial and transaction-related reports through its developer services.

Use Restore Purchases in Settings or on an offer screen to check your entitlements with Apple. Refund requests are handled through Apple's Report a Problem service.

4. Optional rewarded advertising

Blipstorm uses Google AdMob to offer optional ads for a revive or extra coins. Ads only play when you choose to watch one. Blipstorm has no banner or unrequested full-screen ads.

Google and advertising partners may process IP addresses (including approximate location derived from them), device or app identifiers, advertising identifiers where permitted, ad impressions and interactions, and crash or performance information. This is used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and improve services. Blipstorm does not request your precise GPS location for this feature.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, an advertising-consent message explains the available purposes and partners before relevant advertising processing starts. It includes options to decline consent and manage choices. Where Apple's tracking permission is needed, you can choose whether to allow it. Declining tracking does not stop you playing the game. Your choices may affect whether an ad or reward is available.

Where Google requires it, Settings in the game includes AD PRIVACY CHOICES, where you can change or withdraw consent. Apple's tracking permission can also be changed in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Partner choices are described in the consent message.

Google's privacy policy · How Google uses information for advertising · Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures

5. Support messages and this website

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and anything you choose to send, such as a device model, a bug description or a screenshot. This information is used to answer your request, investigate problems and keep necessary correspondence. Email is handled using Google's Gmail service. Please do not include passwords, full payment details or unnecessary personal information.

This website uses GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. The website itself has no contact form, advertising scripts or added analytics, and does not set its own tracking cookies. Following a link to Apple or Google takes you to a service with its own privacy policy.

GitHub's privacy statement

6. Purposes, retention and international processing

Local game information and purchase checks provide the features you request. Support correspondence is handled to respond to you and maintain a working game, relying on legitimate interests and, where applicable, performing a contract. Consent is sought for advertising storage, personalisation or tracking where required. Essential security and legal records may be processed on other applicable lawful bases.

Local data remains until reset or removed from your device, subject to backups. Support correspondence is retained for as long as needed to resolve the request, deal with follow-up issues and meet applicable legal obligations. Apple, Google and GitHub control retention for their own services.

These providers may process information outside the UK or EEA. Their privacy policies explain their international-transfer arrangements, including applicable adequacy frameworks and contractual safeguards. Contact support for information about data handled directly by the developer.

7. Your choices and rights

You may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or transfer personal information, depending on the circumstances. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Email support to make a request. Information held in Apple's, Google's or GitHub's own services may also require a request to that provider.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office or your local data-protection authority. You do not have to contact the developer first.

8. Children and policy changes

Blipstorm is designed for a general audience. It does not ask players to create a developer account or provide a date of birth. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to support, contact the address above. Apple's parental controls can restrict purchases and other features.

This policy will be updated when the app's information practices change. The date at the top identifies the current version.
